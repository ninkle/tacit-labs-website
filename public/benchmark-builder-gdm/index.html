Arena 01 · Applied discovery

Drug discovery and development

Drug discovery and development are organised around a sequence of decisions, from disease and target selection through post-market evidence generation. Each decision is supported by a distinct set of analyses, experiments and regulatory activities. This section groups those activities by the stage at which they are used.

Select the relevant modality, then choose the stages or subtasks to include. The work differs substantially across small molecules, biologics, cell therapies and other modalities.

Arena 02 · Fundamental discovery

Fundamental scientific research

Fundamental research can be represented as a sequence of activities: grounding a question in existing evidence, formulating a falsifiable hypothesis, designing and executing experiments, analysing the resulting data, interpreting the findings, testing their robustness and communicating the conclusions. These stages are not strictly linear and are often repeated as evidence changes.

Select a scientific domain, then choose the research themes to include. A theme is a recurring type of scientific work rather than a single task. We typically author approximately 18 evaluation tasks for each selected theme.

Each theme is labelled by the source of its ground truth. This determines how the task can be constructed and validated:

Expert rubric — reasoning scored against expert-authored criteria Held-out known result Computable ground truth Requires new wet-lab ground truth
